  - quantity: 3

  - quantity: 250

recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Preheat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan).
  - Heat a dash of olive oil in a large frying pan and cook the chicken pieces over
    a high heat for 3 minutes. Add the leek, garlic, soft cheese, mustard, stock cube
    and 200ml boiling water.
  - Pour the mixture into a large ovenproof dish and lay the puff pastry sheet over
    the top.
  - Trim off any excess pastry using a sharp knife. Brush with milk.
  - Bake for 35 minutes or until the top is golden-brown and the pie is cooked through.
    Serve.
