recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - key: coat
    text: Coat the tofu cubes with 2 tablespoons of the olive oil in a bowl, then toss with the
      cornflour, adding a tablespoon at a time.
  - key: fry
    text: Heat just enough oil to cover the bottom of a large non-stick frying pan over a high
      heat. Once the oil is shimmering, fry the tofu until golden on all sides, working in
      batches if needed. This should take around 15 minutes. Set aside on a plate lined with
      kitchen paper to drain.
  - Meanwhile, cook the jasmine rice as per the packet instructions.
  - Stir together the sea salt, white pepper, sugar and garlic powder in a small bowl and
    set aside. In another small bowl, whisk the oyster sauce with 8 tablespoons of water.
  - In another pan, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil over a medium-high heat. Cook
    the white parts of the greens until tender, then stir in the sliced garlic and the
    green parts. Cook until the garlic is soft and the greens have wilted.
  - Stir the oyster sauce mixture into the greens and let it bubble down into a thickened
    sauce; this should only take a couple of minutes.
  - key: season
    text: Just before serving, toss the cooked tofu with the salt and pepper mixture.
  - key: serve
    text: Divide the rice between four warm bowls, then top with the greens and their sauce,
      followed by the tofu and spring onions, if using.
