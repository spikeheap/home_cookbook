recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Preheat the water bath to 80°C.
  - Warm the milk and cream together in a small saucepan to 60°C.
  - In a large bowl, whisk together the egg yolks and sugar until thoroughly
    combined.
  - Slowly whisk the warm cream and milk into the egg mixture.
  - Pour into individual shallow jars and screw on the lids very tightly.
  - Place the jars in the water bath to cook for 60 minutes, then remove from the
    water with tongs and chill them on ice.
  - Once set, sprinkle the surface with demerara sugar and brûlée with a blowtorch.
  - Allow the sugar to set for 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Scale ×1.5 for 6. Make the day before and keep in the fridge in the sealed jars. Only torch just before serving.
- Add a split vanilla pod to the cream as it warms for a classic vanilla version.
