recipeInstructions:
- heading: Ragù (make the day before)
  items:
  - Make a large batch of [ragù alla bolognese](ragu_alla_bolognese.html), scaled
    up to around 1kg beef mince, then cool and refrigerate overnight.
- heading: Spinach pasta (make the day before)
  items:
  - Wilt the spinach in boiling water for 30-60 seconds, drain, cool and squeeze
    very dry.
  - Blend the spinach with the eggs and yolk to a smooth purée.
  - Make a well in the flour and work in the spinach mixture with a fork, then knead
    for 10 minutes until smooth. Wrap and refrigerate; remove 30 minutes before use.
- heading: Béchamel
  items:
  - Melt the butter, add the flour and cook for 1 minute, whisking, to a roux.
  - Whisk in the milk in four additions, add the nutmeg and season. Cook until thick
    enough to coat the back of a spoon, then set aside.
- heading: Assemble and bake
  items:
  - Roll the pasta out thinly, cut to fit the dish, then blanch 2-3 sheets at a time
    for 1 minute and dry on a clean towel.
  - Preheat the oven to 200ºC. Spread a thin layer of ragù over the base of the dish.
  - Layer pasta sheet, ragù, béchamel and Parmigiano, and repeat until used up (at
    least 6 pasta layers), finishing with ragù, béchamel and cheese.
  - Bake for 30 minutes until golden, then rest 10 minutes before serving.
---
Traditional lasagne alla bolognese uses green (spinach) egg pasta sheets, but dried
lasagne sheets work if you'd rather skip the homemade pasta. For a softer bake, La
Cucina Italiana's original uses a gentler 170ºC for 20-25 minutes.
