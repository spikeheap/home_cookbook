recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - key: marinate
    text: Tip the chicken into a large bowl and add the marinade ingredients, along with
      1 tsp salt and plenty of black pepper. Mix well, ensuring each thigh is well
      coated. Cover and chill for at least 3 hours, or up to 48 hours if you have time.
  - A few hours before you want to eat, make the [pitta breads](pitta_wraps.html).
  - To make the tzatziki, halve the cucumber lengthways and scoop out the seeds.
    Finely chop, then combine with the remaining ingredients, along with a pinch of
    salt. Chill until ready to serve.
  - key: thread
    text: Heat the grill to its highest setting. Line a roasting tin with foil and find 4
      metal skewers long enough to sit across the top with a little space underneath.
      Take one thigh and thread it over 2 skewers, so it has a skewer through either
      side. Thread another piece of chicken on top, leaving a slight gap between each
      piece; you should fit 6 thighs on each pair of skewers. Position the skewers on
      top of the roasting tin.
  - key: cook
    text: Put the chicken under the hot grill and cook for 15 to 20 minutes, brushing with
      oil and any juices from the bottom of the tin regularly, and turning halfway
      through cooking. Once cooked, rest for 5 minutes.
  - key: serve
    text: Cut through each pair of skewers to make four kebabs and serve in the warm
      pittas, with lettuce, tomato, red onion, lemon wedges and tzatziki.
