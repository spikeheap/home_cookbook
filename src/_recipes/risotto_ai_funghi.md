recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Bring the water to the boil with the dried porcini and simmer for 20 minutes to
    make a porcini stock (add a stock cube near the end if you like); keep at a gentle
    simmer.
  - Clean the fresh mushrooms (don't wash them), remove woody stalks and tear by hand.
  - Melt 1 tbsp butter with a little oil in a wide pan and fry the fresh mushrooms
    until golden; season, then remove and set aside.
  - In the same pan, melt another tbsp butter with a little oil and sweat the onion
    without browning.
  - Add the rice and toast in the butter for 30-40 seconds, then add the wine and
    let it evaporate.
  - Add the simmering porcini stock a ladle at a time, stirring until absorbed before
    adding the next.
  - After about 10 minutes, stir in the cooked mushrooms and continue adding stock
    until the rice is al dente (about 18 minutes total).
  - Off the heat, beat in a final ladle of stock, the Parmigiano, parsley and the
    remaining chilled butter. Stir vigorously until creamy, adjust salt and rest
    30 seconds before serving.
