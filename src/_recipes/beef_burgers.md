---
date: 2026-06-16
name: Smash burgers
cuisine: American
meal: [Main, Lunch]
effort: weeknight
tags: [beef]
description: Homemade beef patties, keeping it simple.

prepTime: PT20M
cookTime: PT20M
recipeYield: 4 burgers

recipeIngredient:
- heading: Patties
  items:
  - quantity: 800
    unit: g
    item: beef mince (20%+ fat)
  - item: salt and pepper, generously
  - quantity: 2
    unit: tbsp
    item: oil
  - quantity: 3
    item: onions, finely diced
  - quantity: 4
    unit: slices
    item: cheese (Stilton, Kraft slices)
    optional: true
- heading: To assemble
  items:
  - quantity: 4
    item: "[brioche burger buns](quick_brioche_burger_buns.html), lightly toasted"
  - item: lettuce
  - item: tomato, sliced
  - item: pickles, sliced
  - item: mayonnaise
recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Divide the mince into 8 and shape into balls about 5cm wide, handling them lightly. Season both sides generously.
  - Heat 1 tbsp oil in a heavy pan over a high heat and fry the onions until wilted and caramelised; season and set aside.
  - Heat the remaining oil until smoking. Add the patties, flatten them with a heavy press, and cook for 2 minutes undisturbed until a deep crust forms. Flip, cook 1 minute, top with cheese if using, cover and cook 1 more minute until melted.
  - Spread mayonnaise on the toasted bun bases, then layer lettuce, tomato, two patties, onions and pickles.
---
Buns: use the cookbook's [quick](quick_brioche_burger_buns.html) or
[slow](slow_brioche_burger_buns.html) brioche burger buns.
