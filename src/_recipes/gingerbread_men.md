recipeInstructions:
- heading: Biscuits
  items:
  - Sift the flour, bicarb, ginger and cinnamon into a food processor. Add the butter and blitz to fine breadcrumbs, then stir in the sugar. Rub in by hand if you don't have a processor.
  - Beat the egg and golden syrup together, add to the processor and pulse until the dough clumps.
  - Tip out, knead briefly until smooth, wrap and chill for 15 minutes.
  - Preheat the oven to 160°C fan and line two baking trays.
  - Roll out to 5mm on a floured surface, cut out shapes and space them out on the trays.
  - Bake for 12-15 minutes until lightly golden. Leave on the trays for 10 minutes, then cool on a wire rack.
- heading: Icing
  items:
  - Mix the icing sugar with the water, a little at a time, to a thick pipeable paste. Pipe onto the cooled biscuits and add decorations while wet.
