recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Put the chickpeas in a saucepan and mash with a potato masher until as smooth as
    possible.
  - Add the spring onions, cumin, lemon zest, flour and salt. Season with lots of
    black pepper and mix well with a wooden spoon.
  - Using your hands, form the mixture into 16 equal-sized balls and flatten slightly.
  - Heat half the oil in a frying pan over a low heat. Add the falafel (in batches if
    needed) and cook for 4 minutes. Flip, add the rest of the oil and cook for a
    further 3 to 4 minutes, until golden brown and crisp on both sides.
