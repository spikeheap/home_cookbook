recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Heat a wok until smoking and add half the groundnut oil, then add the eggs and scramble
    for 1-2 minutes. Transfer to a warm plate and set aside. Wipe away the excess oil from
    the wok.
  - Reheat the wok and add the remaining oil. When hot, add the broccoli and carrot and
    stir-fry for 3 minutes until tender but still crunchy. Add the rice and stir well to
    break up the grains, then add the tomatoes and stir-fry for 1-2 minutes.
  - Return the egg to the wok and season to taste with the soy sauce, sesame oil and
    pepper. Stir in the sliced spring onion.
  - Pile onto plates and serve immediately.
