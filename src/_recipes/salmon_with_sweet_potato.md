recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Put the wedges on a non-stick baking tray
    and drizzle with 4 tbsp olive oil. Scatter over the rosemary and season, then
    toss together. Spread out on the tray and bake for 25-30 minutes or until tender
    inside and crispy on the outside.
  - Meanwhile, rub the salmon with another splash of oil and dust all over with the
    seasoning. Toss the broccoli in a little oil and season.
  - For the final 10 minutes, add the salmon and broccoli to the tray alongside the
    wedges (use a second tray if it's crowded).
  - Serve the salmon with the wedges and broccoli.
