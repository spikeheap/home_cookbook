We roughly follow this but really just chuck in whatever veg and legumes we have. On a good day we'll take the sausages out of their skins so you have little balls – these hold their shape better. On a _really_ good day we'll breadcrumb the sausage balls and maybe grill them or cook them like dumplings on the top so they have some crunch.
