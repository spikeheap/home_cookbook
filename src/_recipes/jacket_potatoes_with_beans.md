recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Heat the oven to 220ºC (200ºC fan). Rub the potatoes with a little oil and salt,
    place on a baking sheet and bake for 25 minutes.
  - Turn the oven down to 190ºC (170ºC fan) and bake for a further 1 hour to 1 hour
    15 minutes, until the flesh is tender and the skin is crisp and golden.
  - Warm the baked beans in a pan.
  - Cut a cross in the top of each potato and squeeze the base to open it up. Add a
    knob of butter, spoon over the beans and top with grated cheese.
