recipeInstructions:
- items:
  - Heat a pan to a high heat, then add a dash of the oil. When the oil is hot, add
    the cumin seeds, frying until they're almost black. Turn the heat down to medium-low,
    then add the rest of the oil, followed by the onion, garlic and ginger. Cook until
    the onions have turned golden-brown and sweet.
  - Add the fenugreek, garam masala, turmeric and chilli powder (if using), then the
    tomato purée. Cook for a couple of minutes on a high heat, so the tomato purée
    begins to cook through a little.
  - Pour in 500ml water. Add the salt and sugar, bring to the boil and remove from
    the heat.
  - For a silky smooth sauce, blend with a stick blender or food processor for about
    a minute. Stir in the crème fraîche.
  - Return the pan to the heat, add the peas and paneer, and simmer on medium for
    around 10 minutes, or until the cheese has softened.
  - Turn off the heat and gently stir in the butter.
  - Garnish with chopped coriander and pomegranate seeds. Serve with rice.
