swap:
  protein:
  - quantity: 2
    item: courgettes, halved lengthways and cut into 2cm pieces
  - quantity: 500
    unit: g
    item: halloumi, sliced
  - quantity: 90
    unit: g
    item: green pesto
  stock: []
  assemble: Put everything except the halloumi and pesto into a large roasting tin. Toss
    in the oil until well coated, then spread out in a single layer.
  finish: Toss the veg, lay the halloumi slices on top and roast for a further 15 to 20
    minutes, until the halloumi is golden at the edges. Loosen the pesto with a little
    olive oil and drizzle over to serve.
